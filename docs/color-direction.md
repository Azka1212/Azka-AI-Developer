# Color direction — September 29, 2026

Inspected live homepages in Chrome at 1440 × 1000 with a light browser color scheme. Recorded screenshots and computed styles. This is a comparison of three sites, not a claim that the entire AI industry follows one trend.

| Site | Sampled page background | Sampled main text | Observed treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/) | #FAF9F5 | #141413 | Warm neutral surfaces, near-black text and controls; imagery carries much of the color. |
| [Cursor](https://cursor.com/) | #F7F7F4 | #26251E | Off-white canvas, warm dark text, pale inset surfaces, dark primary controls. |
| [Cohere](https://cohere.com/) | #FFFFFF | #212121 on headings | Neutral interface, dark actions, color concentrated in the logo and large imagery. Body default is black. |

Lovable was also attempted, but its browser verification page prevented visual inspection; it was not used as evidence. Perplexity did not expose useful visual information through the available web response.

## Applied decision

Keep the approved profile/sidebar layout and all content. Use a unified warm-stone base rather than a large saturated sidebar. Maintain the larger typography and stronger contrast introduced in the previous revision.

- Main canvas: #F3F1EB
- Sidebar: #E7E3DA
- Cards: #FAF8F3
- Inset panels: #EEEBE3
- Main text and selected navigation: #24231F
- Secondary text: #555249
- Dividers: #D3CEC3
- Links and keyboard focus: #9C3E25

The burnt-orange accent is a portfolio-specific choice, not a sampled brand color. It is limited mainly to links and focus states. Active navigation is dark neutral with light text; badges and icons are neutral. Palette-switcher UI and its saved-preference behavior have been removed. Existing browser palette preferences no longer affect the page because alternate palette selectors were removed.
